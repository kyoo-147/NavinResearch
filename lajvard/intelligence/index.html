INTELLIGENCE / BOUNDARIES

Helpful does not mean all-knowing.

Lajvard may combine perception, memory, and language someday. The system must show uncertainty instead of performing confidence.

NOTE 01RESEARCH DIRECTION

Three modest layers.

We are exploring a perception layer, a local journal, and an interaction layer. Their interfaces and models remain research questions.

  • Perception can be wrong
  • Memory is owner-controlled
  • Responses are not evidence of understanding
NOTE 02BOUNDARY

Warmth without a claim of feeling.

A consistent voice can make an interface legible; it cannot establish sentience, emotion, or a human relationship.

  • Personality is authored behavior
  • No sentience claim
  • Uncertainty stays visible
