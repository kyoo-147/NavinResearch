A companion project, kept accountable.

Lajvard is a Navin Research product direction exploring how robotics can enter daily life with warmth, restraint, and explicit boundaries.

01

Useful wonder, without theater.

We will separate sketches from prototypes, demos from dependable behavior, and intentions from shipped capabilities.

  • Source-backed writing

  • Human judgment at the center

A product direction within Navin Research.

CONTEXT

  • No manufactured maturity
