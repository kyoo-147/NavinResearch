THE ROBOT / MOVEMENT NOTES

Movement should earn its place.

A robot in a home needs safe pauses, clear intent, and an honest answer when the route is unknown.

BEHAVIOR STUDY

A sequence, not a promise.

  1. 01

    A question, not a benchmark.

    Public robotics work demonstrates many approaches to mobility, but none of it is evidence that Lajvard moves. We are studying how movement can feel calm and predictable.

    NOT DEMONSTRATED
    • Navigation is not demonstrated
    • No speed, range, or autonomy claim
    • Pause and ask when uncertain
  2. 02

    The quiet behavior matters most.

    Any future movement system needs an explicit stop state and a way for a person to understand what the robot intends to do.

    RESEARCH QUESTION
    • Stop must be visible
    • Uncertainty must interrupt action
    • Physical behavior requires physical evidence
