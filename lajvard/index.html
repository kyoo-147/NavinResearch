Life with a little presence.

Lajvard begins with the room: a quiet hello, a small ritual, a companion that knows the limits of what it knows. This is a concept, not a shipping announcement.

Designed around ordinary moments

The first question is not how much a robot can do. It is whether its presence makes a familiar day feel more attentive without becoming intrusive.

CONCEPT STAGE
  • A companion for small rituals
  • Visible boundaries around memory and listening
  • No claim of sentience or emotional understanding
