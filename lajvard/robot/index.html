THE ROBOT / FORM STUDY

A small body for a real room.

The form is intentionally open: compact, approachable, and capable of being present without taking over the room.

NOTE 01CONCEPT STAGE

Character before specification.

Lajvard explores a humanoid silhouette because face, posture, and gesture can make a machine legible. That is a design hypothesis, not a capability claim.

  • Compact humanoid direction
  • Warm character, not childish branding
  • Physical dimensions and materials are not final
NOTE 02UNKNOWN

A body is a set of compromises.

The body must eventually account for power, repair, sound, safe movement, and ownership. No component choice has been announced.

  • No prototype shown
  • No certification claimed
  • No physical specification published
