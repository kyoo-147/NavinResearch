ABOUT / OUR STRUCTURE

Our structure

Navin Research works as a small independent research organization. This public structure describes how the work is organized; it is not a statement of corporate form or legal governance.

01

Direction

The founder sets the institution’s research direction and connects long-term questions to the work that can be examined now.

02

Research programs

Machine learning and language model research are organized around explicit questions, inspectable evidence, and clear limits.

03

Product systems

Research is translated into distinct product systems without treating concepts, demonstrations, or plans as validated capability.
