EVALUATION

Describe the listening conditions.

Language coverage is inseparable from acoustic scene, terminology, speaker turns, and the review standard applied to an output.

01

A language claim needs a protocol.

A future evaluation record should identify the audio conditions, language context, terminology, expected output, and who reviewed disagreements. Moyi publishes no language list or score here.

EVALUATION PROTOCOL: NOT PUBLISHED
  • Record the scene and turn boundaries
  • Declare terminology and target context
  • Keep disagreement and unknowns visible
02

Context is not a footnote.

A clean transcript in one room does not establish performance in another. Conditions must travel with the observation.

  • Scene metadata
  • Review criteria
  • Limitations
