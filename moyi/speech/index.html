Hear the scene, not only the words.

Distance, noise, turn-taking, and local terminology shape speech work. Moyi keeps those conditions visible for study and review.

ACOUSTIC FIELD

Keep state, context, and review separate.

  1. 01

    Context is part of the input.

    Moyi's speech direction treats the surrounding acoustic field as research context. Quality and coverage remain to be measured.

    UNVERIFIED
  2. 02

    A clean transcript is not the whole scene.

    Review should preserve what was audible, what was inferred, and what remained unclear instead of collapsing conditions into one text output.

    SCENE RECORD: REQUIRED
  3. 03

    Describe the room.

    Distance, overlap, noise, and turn boundaries belong in a speech record. They are conditions to measure, not promises of quality.
