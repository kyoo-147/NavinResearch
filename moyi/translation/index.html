TRANSLATION

Translate the operational meaning.

A translation runtime should leave room for names, procedures, safety wording, and human review.

01

Terminology is part of safety.

The product direction is to preserve team vocabulary rather than treat every utterance as generic text. No language-count or quality claim is announced.

  • Names and terms
  • Procedure context
  • Human confirmation
02

Meaning needs a review boundary.

A translated phrase can be useful without being automatically actionable. The interface should distinguish a proposed rendering from a reviewed handoff.

REVIEW STATE: EXPLICIT
03

Carry the lexicon through.

Names, procedures, and safety phrases should remain visible from capture through translation and review.
