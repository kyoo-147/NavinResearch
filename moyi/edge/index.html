Keep the critical path close.

Local execution is the intended direction for conditions where connectivity, privacy, or response requirements vary.

01

Deployment boundaries stay explicit.

Moyi does not claim a shipped edge package. Hardware support, resource requirements, and quality remain open questions.

  • What enters the device
  • What stays local
  • What may leave
02

Local is a boundary, not a guarantee.

A future edge record should name hardware, runtime configuration, resource limits, and output controls before making a deployment statement.

EDGE PACKAGE: NOT ANNOUNCED
03

The device is a boundary.

Local processing changes what leaves the device, but hardware, resource, and quality requirements still need evidence.
