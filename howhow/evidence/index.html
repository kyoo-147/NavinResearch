EVIDENCE / LEDGER

Every claim gets a label, not a halo.

Sources, spans, artifacts, and unresolved uncertainty remain inspectable instead of disappearing into polished prose.

01

Source records are not vibes.

A source needs a stable identity, version, retrieval context, access and license status, and a locator that lets another reader find the supporting passage.

SOURCE RECORD
  • Stable ID
  • Version
  • Exact locator
02

Claims carry their ancestry.

External claims point to evidence spans. Empirical claims point to run manifests and metrics. Interpretive claims and hypotheses remain labelled as such.

CLAIM TYPE
  • External
  • Empirical
  • Interpretive / hypothesis
03

Unknown is a valid entry.

Contradictions, unsupported hypotheses, and dissent are not collapsed into a decorative confidence badge. The ledger shows what is missing.

FAIL CLOSED
  • Supported
  • Contradicted
  • UNKNOWN
