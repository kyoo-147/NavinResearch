ABOUT / REVIEW STAMP

Apply for a review, not a magic button.

HowHow is presented as READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW / integration spike. Access, scope, and implementation path remain subject to human review.

01

The gate asks for a real question.

An access conversation should include the research question, intended inputs, permitted environment, decision owner, and what a useful bounded episode would produce.

APPLICATION INPUT
  • Question
  • Inputs
  • Decision
02

The stamp has a narrow meaning.

READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW means the direction is ready to discuss as an integration spike. It does not mean generally available, proven, novel, correct, or accepted.

READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW
  • Not GA
  • Not a result
  • Not publication
03

Human review comes before work.

No service, pricing, customer list, or delivery commitment is announced. Scope and permissions would be agreed before any episode begins.

AVAILABILITY UNANNOUNCED
  • Review
  • Approve
  • Preserve
