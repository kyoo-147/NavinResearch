PACKET ANATOMY

Brief → sources → claims → checks → decision.

  1. 01

    Start with an editable brief.

    Natural-language intent becomes a versioned question, scope, non-goals, permissions, budget, stop policy, and expected artifacts before research work begins.

    BRIEF REQUIRED
  2. 02

    Keep the trail beside the prose.

    Sources, exact spans, runs, artifacts, claims, and review records remain linked. A polished paragraph cannot erase an unsupported path.

    PROVENANCE FIRST
  3. 03

    End at a gate, not a button.

    The package can be checked for rebuildability, license, privacy, and manuscript consistency, then handed to a person for a final decision.
