PROJECTS / THE SHELF

One project, one bowl, one accountable trail.

Projects keep a question, its evidence, and its approvals together without implying that work has been completed.

01

A shelf, not a success gallery.

Each project entry should expose status, owner, scope, evidence count, open blockers, and the next human-owned decision.

02

Episodes can pause without disappearing.

Pause, take over, stop-and-preserve, cancel, failed, and inconclusive are durable states-not blank spaces hidden behind a green check.

  • BLOCKED

The package is a handoff.
