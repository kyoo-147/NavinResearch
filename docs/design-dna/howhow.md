# HowHow design DNA

## Reference reconstruction

Reference study covered the public HowHow-Reasoner repository plus FutureHouse/PaperQA, Hebbia, OffLimits, and Goodles. The useful patterns are adapted, not copied: research products make the work legible through source trails, project/workspace states, review surfaces, and deep documentation; Goodles and OffLimits demonstrate that packaging, ingredient language, and shelf-style product storytelling can make a category memorable. HowHow combines those lessons into an original research packet and evidence ledger.

## Information architecture

- `/` — thesis, packet metaphor, status, and evidence boundary
- `/product/` — canonical system model: brief → ledger → review → handoff
- `/how-it-works/` — five-stage user journey
- `/research-workflow/` — pre-run policy, bounded execution, preserved receipt
- `/evidence/` — source, claim, audit, and unknown states
- `/experiments/` — protocol, run receipt, interpretation limits
- `/projects/` — lifecycle shelf and accountable episodes
- `/docs/`, `/github/`, `/releases/` — source, contract, and artifact trail
- `/access/`, `/contact/` — human gate and bounded intake
- `/about/`, `/privacy/`, `/terms/` — identity and boundaries

Each route has a distinct page purpose, visual state, and section sequence. No page claims live runs, customer results, novelty, pricing, or automatic publication.

## Direction

Serious research system in intentionally funny instant-noodle/FMCG packaging. The joke is the wrapper; the evidence boundary is never a joke. Original work only: no copied logos, code, packaging artwork, or screenshots from references.

## Typography

Use Georgia for human-readable narrative and Arial/system sans for labels, statuses, navigation, and evidence metadata. Sentence case for headlines; uppercase is reserved for short labels and package stamps.

## Palette

- Paper: `#101714` (deep green-black)
- Ink: `#f2eee4` (warm paper)
- Signal: `#d5ff55` (acid noodle-lime)
- Warning: `#ff8066` (tomato)
- Dim: `#aaa99f` (muted annotation)

## Primitives

The product uses a tilted noodle packet, back-of-pack workflow, ingredient-list rows, source/claim ledger, review stamp, failure receipt, project shelf, and source trail. Rules and stamps replace generic SaaS cards. Route-specific `data-kind` sections use red exception rails for policy/boundary, ledger washes for evidence, and receipt/shelf compositions for artifacts.

## Responsive and access

Use generous editorial gutters and a 12-column desktop rhythm that collapses cleanly at 800px and 500px. Preserve semantic headings, keyboard focus, readable contrast, touch-sized controls, and reduced-motion behavior. Validate at 375, 768, 1440, and wide desktop widths.

## Truth boundary

Keep `READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW` and `integration spike` visible. Label concept diagrams as concept direction, never as product screenshots or physical proof. `UNKNOWN`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `BLOCKED`, and human publication decisions remain first-class states.

## Prohibited patterns

No frontier-AI clone, aurora/mesh gradients, fake dashboards, invented metrics, unlabelled testimonials, generic SaaS pricing cards, copied reference assets, or heading-only routes. Every route needs a meaningful explanation and explicit evidence/status boundary.
