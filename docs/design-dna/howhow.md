# HowHow design DNA

## Direction
Serious research system in intentionally funny instant-noodle/FMCG packaging. The joke is the wrapper; the evidence boundary is never a joke. Original work only: no copied logos, code, packaging artwork, or screenshots from references.

## Typography
Use Georgia (or the shared editorial serif) for human-readable narrative and Arial/system sans for labels, statuses, navigation, and evidence metadata. Sentence case for headlines; uppercase is reserved for short labels.

## Palette
- Paper: `#101714` (deep green-black)
- Ink: `#f2eee4` (warm paper)
- Signal: `#d5ff55` (acid noodle-lime)
- Warning: `#ff8066` (tomato)
- Dim: `#aaa99f` (muted annotation)

## Spacing and primitives
Use generous editorial gutters and a 12-column desktop rhythm that collapses cleanly at 768px and 375px. Primary primitives are a noodle pack, a small reviewer mascot, ingredient-list rows, evidence ledger, review stamp, source trail, and shelf-like release index. Prefer borders, rules, stamps, and diagrams to generic card grids.

## Motion and accessibility
Motion is optional and subordinate to reading. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; preserve visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, link navigation, contrast, and touch-sized controls. Test at 375, 768, 1440, and large desktop widths.

## Truth boundary
Keep `READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW` and `integration spike` visible. Label concept diagrams as concept direction, never as product screenshots or physical proof. Do not claim availability, prices, customers, benchmarks, certifications, novelty, or publication outcomes without a named source.

## Prohibited patterns
No frontier-AI clone, aurora/mesh gradients, fake dashboards, invented metrics, unlabelled testimonials, generic SaaS pricing cards, copied reference assets, or heading-only routes. Every route needs a meaningful explanation and an explicit evidence/status boundary.

## Reference posture
FutureHouse, Hebbia, OffLimits, Goodles, and the public HowHow-Reasoner repository are reference points for inspection only. HowHow borrows no protected visual identity; its own visual system is the noodle-pack/evidence-ledger metaphor grounded in the public repository's stated direction.
