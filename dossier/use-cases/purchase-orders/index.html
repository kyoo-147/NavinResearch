DOSSIER / DOCUMENT INTELLIGENCE

Make commitments inspectable.

A purchase-order workflow can preserve line-item context while preparing a record for downstream reconciliation.

  1. 01

    A record with its original page

    The useful unit is not a detached field. It is a field, its evidence, and the decision that accepted it.

    CONCEPT / SOURCE-BOUND
    1. Order identifiers
    2. Line-item context
    3. Approval status
  2. 02

    Compare commitments without detaching them from the page.

    The useful unit is not a detached field. It is a field, its evidence, and the decision that accepted it. The page stays deliberately separate from a live processing result: this is a product direction and interface contract, not a performance claim.

    1. Source context stays adjacent
    2. Uncertainty remains addressable
    3. A reviewer can stop the handoff
  3. 03

    Commitments need comparison context.

    Purchase-order review preserves identifiers, requested quantities, prices, and approval history before a comparison is treated as resolved.

    ILLUSTRATIVE PATH
    1. Order identity
    2. Quantity and price
    3. Approval history
