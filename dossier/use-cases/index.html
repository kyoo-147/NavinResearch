  2. 02

    Choose a workflow by the decision the document must support.

    The right workflow depends on the document, the rule, and the person accountable for the outcome. The page stays deliberately separate from a live processing result: this is a product direction and interface contract, not a performance claim.

    1. Source context stays adjacent
    2. Uncertainty remains addressable
    3. A reviewer can stop the handoff
  3. 03

    The document serves a decision.

    Different teams need different evidence: payment, commitment, exception handling, or an audit trail. Start with that decision rather than a generic extraction promise.

    WORKFLOW DIRECTION
    1. Decision
    2. Required evidence
    3. Accountable reviewer
