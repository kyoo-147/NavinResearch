OPERATING THESIS

Responsibility before automation.

Sandora is an early product direction for coordinating virtual AI teams. It treats roles, context, handoffs, approvals, and audit as the connective tissue of automated workflows—not as hidden implementation detail.

Concept-stage description only. No production deployment, performance claim, pricing, customer result, certification, or physical proof is being asserted.
