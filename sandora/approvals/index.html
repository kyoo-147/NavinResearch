06 / GOVERNANCE

The stop is part of the system.

An approval is a state transition owned by a person: the proposal, context, decision, and next action should remain attached. Sandora does not claim autonomous authority or production enforcement.

  1. 01

    Decision record

    An approval captures proposal, context, decision, and accountable owner as one inspectable record.

    CONCEPT-STAGE
    1. Proposal
    2. Context attached
    3. Human owner
    Review the record →
  2. 02

    After the gate

    The workflow resumes only from an explicit state, never from an implied yes.

    CONCEPT-STAGE
    1. Approved
    2. Rejected
    3. Needs context

Keep the boundary visible.

Sandora is an early concept. Contact is an expression of interest, not a promise of access.
